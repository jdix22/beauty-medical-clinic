Organic Herbal Peel in Thornhill

A gentle but effective way to step up your skincare: the organic herbal peel counteracts summer sun damage, improves tone and texture, speeds cell renewal and helps with acne and scarring.

What is in it

The peel is a blend of finely crushed whole plants, salicylic acid and multivitamins. Its key plant is Spongilla fluviatilis, a freshwater sponge also known as badyaga, which is rich in silica, lime and alumina. Silica supports healthy collagen; alumina and lime boost circulation.

Peel care instructions

Before

  • Avoid sun, tanning beds and self-tanner for at least 24 hours before, ideally a week.
  • Stop Accutane and other photosensitizing medication at least 6 months before and during treatment.
  • No other chemical peel on the area for 2 weeks before.
  • No Retin-A, Renova or Tazorac for 1 week before.
  • Not during pregnancy.
  • Arrive with clean skin: no lotion, makeup or sunscreen.

After

  • Avoid sun for at least 24 hours, ideally 1–2 weeks. Hat and SPF 30+ for incidental sun.
  • Wash gently twice a day with a mild cleanser and lukewarm water.
  • Moisturize with a soothing cream as often as needed for 3 days.
  • Let old skin flake off naturally: no picking or exfoliating.
  • No exfoliating products for at least 1 week, and no other peel for 2 weeks.
  • No Retin-A, Renova or Tazorac for 1 week.
  • No sweaty exercise, hot tub, sauna or steam for 5 days if there is irritation.
  • No makeup for 12 hours.

Organic herbal peel FAQ

How many herbal peels do I need?

You will see a difference after one, but the best result comes from one peel every two weeks for two months.
