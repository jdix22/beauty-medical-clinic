Five rules for a beautiful lip result

01

Choose an injector you trust

Experience comes first. A good injector answers every question, explains your options, and has done a great many lips.

02

Choose the right filler

There are many kinds. If you are new to filler, a hyaluronic acid product such as Juvéderm, Restylane or Emervel is the sensible start: if you don’t love it, an enzyme called hyaluronidase dissolves it.

03

Be conservative

Too little is always better than too much. Start small and build toward your goal. If people can tell you’ve had filler, it has probably gone too far.

04

Be specific

Almost everyone asks for “natural”, but that means something different to each person. Describe exactly what you want so expectations are clear.

05

Keep expectations realistic

Bring inspiration photos, but very full lips don’t suit every face. Victoria will advise what will look right on yours.

Not sure yet? Try it for an evening first

If you have wondered whether a fuller pout would suit you, the Cinderella lips treatment uses sterile saline to give a temporary plump that fades within hours, so you can see the look before committing.

Lip fillers FAQ

How long do lip fillers last?

Most last about 6 to 12 months, depending on your body and how active you are.

Can lip filler be undone?

Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with an injection of hyaluronidase if you dislike the result.
