Privacy policy

How Beauty Medical Clinic handles information you share through this website.

What we collect

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you send the appointment request form, it opens your own email program addressed to info@beautymedicalclinic.com with the details you typed. We receive only what you choose to send: usually your name, phone number, email address, the treatment you are interested in and your message.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and arrange your appointment. We do not sell or share them. Health information you discuss at the clinic is handled under Ontario’s Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA).

Third-party content

The contact page shows a Google Map, and some links open WhatsApp or Google Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Questions

Contact us at info@beautymedicalclinic.com or 416-728-5697.
