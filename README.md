# Beauty Medical Clinic: website redesign

Redesign of beautymedicalclinic.com for Victoria Adamov, RN (Thornhill), built November 2026.
All treatment information, contact details and photos come from the clinic's current WordPress site.

Live site: https://beautymedical.ca/ (Cloudflare Worker "beauty-medical-clinic", launched 7 Nov 2026)
GitHub Pages copy: https://jdix22.github.io/beauty-medical-clinic/ (its canonical points at beautymedical.ca)

Redeploy after any change: `node _src/build.js`, then `wrangler deploy` from this folder.
IndexNow key: `58994ea5125f1c575f3ee18dbaa497fc.txt` (all 22 pages sent 7 Nov 2026; re-send after big changes).
Pushing to GitHub only updates the github.io copy.

## What changed from the old site

- 16 treatment pages, each with quick facts (time, sessions, downtime, results), plain-language
  sections, before/after photos, preparation and aftercare, FAQ, and related treatments.
  The separate FAQ and aftercare pages were folded into the treatment they belong to.
- A "face map" on the home page: tap an area of the face to see which treatments address it.
- Every old address (e.g. `/botox-dysport-faq/`, `/lumeca/`, `/news/`) forwards to its new home,
  so existing Google results and links keep working.
- Tap-to-call, text and WhatsApp on every page, plus a request form that pre-selects the treatment.
- Search and AI-answer groundwork: unique titles and descriptions, structured data for the clinic,
  Victoria and each treatment, visible FAQs, a sitemap, social preview image.
- Open to Google and AI search on beautymedical.ca (no `noindex`). AI crawlers are allowed.
- `worker.js` forces https and sends www.beautymedical.ca to beautymedical.ca. Old WordPress
  addresses get real 301 redirects from `_redirects` (generated by the build).

### Design refresh (11 Nov 2026)

Victoria asked for a more "beautiful" look than competing clinics (reviewed: Toula, Victoria Park
Medispa, Canada MedLaser, SFP Medispa). Changes: Cormorant + Jost type, ivory/teal/champagne-gold
palette, editorial split hero, philosophy section, face map in its own section, arch-framed photos,
photo-backed booking band. All features and wording kept.

Mood photos from Unsplash (free licence, commercial use, no credit needed): `serum-dropper-skin`,
`clinical-skin-care-gloves`, `facial-mask-treatment` (Salt & Silk card), `handheld-device-facial`
(Forma card), `soft-focus-skin-closeup` (booking band). They are illustrative, not clinic clients.
Biggest remaining upgrade: a professional portrait of Victoria (the current one is a close selfie)
and a few photos of the treatment room.

Business-card QR codes: `../beauty-medical-clinic-print/` (PNG, SVG, and a labelled PNG). They point
to https://beautymedical.ca.

### Wording that was softened on purpose

Health advertising should avoid absolute or unproven claims, so these old-site phrases were
not carried over: "absolutely safe, no risk, no side effects" (Cinderella), "FDA approved" (PRP),
"permanently kill fat" and "zero non-responders" (BodyFX), "results up to 5–10 years" (Forma),
"the Number One place". The facts behind each treatment are unchanged.
