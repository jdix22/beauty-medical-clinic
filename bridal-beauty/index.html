Your bridal beauty timeline

When should you start preparing?

Enter your wedding date to see where you are on the timeline.

The stage you’re in will light up below.

Book a bridal consultation

  1. 3–6 months before

    A great time to discuss your skin concerns, explore treatment options and create a personalized beauty plan.

  2. 4–8 weeks before

    Depending on your skin and treatment goals, this may be an appropriate time for selected skin rejuvenation treatments that require recovery or multiple appointments.

  3. 1–2 weeks before

    Focus on familiar, gentle treatments that your skin has previously tolerated well. Avoid experimenting with aggressive or unfamiliar procedures close to the event.

  4. Wedding week

    Keep your skincare routine simple, gentle and focused on hydration and comfort.

Treatment timing is individualized. Some procedures require longer recovery periods, and results cannot be guaranteed by a particular date.
