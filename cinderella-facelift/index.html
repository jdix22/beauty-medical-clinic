Cinderella Facelift & Lips in Thornhill

A temporary saline plump for the lips, cheeks or other areas that lasts about one evening, then fades. It is ideal for a wedding, party, photo shoot or date, and an inexpensive way to try a fuller look before you commit to longer-lasting filler.

Why it is called Cinderella

Like Cinderella’s evening at the ball, the effect wears off after the night is over. Sterile saline is injected along the lip line, into the cheeks or other parts of the face, the change is visible straight away, and it fades within about eight to twelve hours.

The trend started in Europe, and Beauty Medical Clinic was among the first clinics in the Toronto area to introduce Cinderella Lips and Cinderella Facelifts.

Who it is for

“Many women tell me they’ve thought about lip or cheek fillers but were never sure whether a fuller pout or more defined features would be right for them. This lets them try it first, see how friends and family react, and grow comfortable with the change.”

Victoria Adamov, RN
  • Weddings, galas, holiday parties and New Year’s
  • Photo sessions and special occasions
  • Anyone curious about filler who wants to see the look first

Inexpensive, but still a medical treatment

It is quick and simple, but it is still an injection. Victoria’s advice: choose someone experienced and properly qualified, with a good eye for facial anatomy, who will tell you when enough is enough. The result should look natural and enhance your features rather than change them.

Cinderella facelift FAQ

How long do Cinderella lips last?

About eight to twelve hours, which is enough for one event.

Is it the same as filler?

No. It uses sterile saline, which the body absorbs within hours. Hyaluronic acid filler lasts months. Many people try Cinderella first, then move on to filler.
