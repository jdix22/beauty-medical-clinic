Mesotherapy in Thornhill

Mesotherapy delivers a cocktail of hyaluronic acid, vitamins, minerals and amino acids into the middle layer of the skin through a series of very fine injections. It nourishes and hydrates while stimulating the collagen and elastin that keep skin firm.

Maintenance

What to expect

It can be slightly uncomfortable, so numbing cream is available for sensitive skin. Any small bruises or swelling settle within about 24 hours. Over the course, skin becomes more radiant, hydrated and firm, with better texture.

Benefits

  • Brightens dull, tired-looking skin and softens superficial wrinkles
  • Improves sluggish circulation
  • Helps with pigmentation and acne scarring
  • Boosts the results of Botox or Dysport, fillers, microneedling and laser

Finished with red LED

Every mesotherapy session at the clinic ends with red LED light therapy. Red light supports the skin barrier, speeds healing, calms inflammation and encourages new collagen. It also acts on the oil glands to reduce the inflammatory signals behind chronic acne.

Mesotherapy FAQ

How many mesotherapy sessions do I need?

A course of four to six sessions, every two weeks, then maintenance once or twice a year.
