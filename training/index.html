Hands-on aesthetics training for doctors and nurses

Rebecca Victoria Adam, RN, Certified Aesthetic Injector, has taught medical professionals for more than ten years: hundreds of physicians and registered nurses from Australia, Europe and North America have learned her techniques.

01

Botox & Dysport

Injection technique for the upper face: muscle anatomy, injection points, dosing and avoiding complications such as brow or lid ptosis.

02

Dermal fillers

Hyaluronic acid filler placement for lips, cheeks, folds, chin and jawline, with an emphasis on natural, anatomy-led results.

03

Sclerotherapy

Treating spider and reticular veins: patient selection, technique, compression and aftercare.

Course dates and enrolment
