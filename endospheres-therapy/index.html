Endospheres Therapy in Thornhill

Endospheres is an Italian technology that uses compressive microvibration: a roller of 55 silicone spheres sends low-frequency vibrations deep into the tissue. It boosts blood and lymph circulation and tones muscle, helping with cellulite, fluid retention, muscle tension and loss of facial definition, and it is genuinely relaxing.

Five actions in one treatment

01

Analgesic

Stimulates the skin’s receptors to ease discomfort.

02

Draining

The rotating cylinder activates lymphatic drainage, so you feel lighter and less puffy.

03

Vascular

“Vascular gymnastics” improve microcirculation for a more even look.

04

Remodelling

Compressing the tissue restores elasticity and definition.

05

Muscular

Vibration makes muscles contract, toning and reshaping them.

Endospheres body

Massage rollers and gentle vacuum improve circulation and drainage, so skin becomes smoother and firmer and cellulite less visible.

  • Tightens loose skin
  • Reduces cellulite, even at an advanced stage when the orange-peel look shows without pinching
  • Drains lactic acid and relieves muscle spasms, which makes it great after exercise
  • Stimulates collagen and elastin
  • Helps reshape the figure after giving birth

Endospheres FAQ

Is Endospheres painful?

No. It is non-invasive and most clients find it relaxing, like a deep rhythmic massage.

Is it for men too?

Yes. Endospheres is used on both women and men, for the body and the face.
